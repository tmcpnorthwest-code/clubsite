# D3 Toastmasters — Pathways Level Requirements

**Source:** [D3 Toastmasters: What's Now Mandatory to Complete Your Pathways Levels](https://d3toastmasters.org/whats-now-mandatory-to-complete-your-pathways-levels/)
**Effective:** October 2025 onwards
**Club:** Our club tracks all mandatory roles through the member portal.

---

## Important Rules That Apply to ALL Levels

1. **Roles must be completed within your current level.** A role done at Level 1 does NOT count toward Level 2 requirements, and so on. You must repeat it.
2. **Progress before October 2025 remains valid** and is carried forward.
3. **Educational Presentations require club officer validation in Base Camp.**
4. You must be an active, paid-up member to be assigned roles.

## Role Eligibility by Level

### Meeting Roles

The table below shows the **minimum level you must have completed** before you can be assigned a meeting role.

| Role | Minimum Level Completed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sergeant at Arms | Any (L0+) | Open to all members including new |
| Active Listener | Any (L0+) | Open to all members |
| Timer | Any (L0+) | First mandatory at L1 |
| Ah-Counter | Any (L0+) | First mandatory at L1 |
| Evaluator | Any (L0+) | First mandatory at L1 |
| Table Topics Speaker | Any (L0+) | Must be done BEFORE Ice Breaker at L1 |
| Grammarian | L1 completed | First mandatory at L2 |
| Table Topics Master | L1 completed | First mandatory at L2 |
| Toastmaster of the Day (TMOD) | **L2 completed** | First mandatory at L3 |
| General Evaluator (GE) | **L2 completed** | First mandatory at L4 |

### Mentorship Roles

Mentorship roles are **not meeting roles**. They are assigned by the VPE outside the meeting agenda. To be eligible as any mentor, you must be an active, paid-up member who has completed Level 2.

| Role | Eligibility | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Introductory Mentor | L2 completed + active + paid | Guide a new member through a single meeting. Assigned by VPE. Counts as the L3 optional role requirement. |
| Club Mentor | L2 completed + active + paid | Full ongoing mentor for a new member. Assigned by VPE. Counts as an L5 optional role requirement. |

## What You Need to Complete Each Level

### Level 1 — Building Your Foundation

**Goal:** Establish your communication baseline and understand meeting roles.

| # | Requirement | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Table Topics Speaker | Role | **Must be done BEFORE your Ice Breaker speech** |
| 2 | Evaluator | Role | Evaluate another member's speech |
| 3 | Timer **OR** Ah-Counter | Role | Complete at least one of these |
| 4 | Ice Breaker speech | Speech | Your first 4–6 minute speech (after TT Speaker) |
| 5 | 4 more path speeches | Speeches | Total of 5 speeches to complete L1 |

**Note:** The ordering constraint is strict — you cannot deliver your Ice Breaker until you have completed the Table Topics Speaker role.

---

### Level 2 — Understanding Your Style

**Goal:** Develop leadership versatility and serve in facilitation roles.

| # | Requirement | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grammarian | Role | Track and report on language use |
| 2 | Table Topics Master | Role | Facilitate the impromptu speaking segment |
| 3 | Evaluator | Role | Must be done again at this level |
| 4 | Toastmaster of the Day (TMOD) **OR** Timer **OR** Ah-Counter | Role | Complete at least one of these |
| 5 | 3 path speeches | Speeches | Total of 3 speeches at L2 |

### Level 3 — Putting Skills Into Action

**Goal:** Take on advanced facilitation and begin mentoring.

| # | Requirement | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Toastmaster of the Day (TMOD) | Role | Full meeting facilitation |
| 2 | Evaluator | Role | Must be done again at this level |
| 3 | Table Topics Master **OR** Table Topics Speaker **OR** Introductory Mentor | Role / Mentorship | Complete at least one. Introductory Mentor is a VPE-assigned mentorship role, not a meeting slot — notify VPE to arrange. |
| 4 | Educational Presentation — Successful Club Series | Presentation | 10–15 minute presentation; must be validated in Base Camp |
| 5 | 3 path speeches | Speeches | Total of 3 speeches at L3 |

### Level 4 — Making a Bigger Impact

**Goal:** Lead evaluation and contribute club-wide educational content.

| # | Requirement | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | General Evaluator (GE) | Role | Lead the full evaluation segment |
| 3 | Evaluator | Role | Must be done again at this level |

| 5 | Educational Presentation — Successful Club Series | Presentation | 10–15 minute; Base Camp validation |

### Level 5 — Demonstrating Expertise

**Goal:** Demonstrate sustained leadership across all meeting dimensions.

| # | Requirement | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General Evaluator (GE) | Role | **Twice** at this level |
| 2 | Toastmaster of the Day (TMOD) | Role | **Twice** at this level |
| 3 | Evaluator | Role | **Twice** at this level |
| 4 | One from: TT Speaker, TT Master, Ah-Counter, Timer, Grammarian, **Club Mentor**, Specialized Role, or repeat above | Role / Mentorship | Meeting roles are tracked via agenda. Club Mentor is a VPE-assigned mentorship role — notify VPE separately. |
| 5 | Educational Presentation — Successful Club Series | Presentation | 10–15 minute; Base Camp validation |
| 6 | Educational Presentation — Leadership Excellence Series | Presentation | 10–15 minute; Base Camp validation |
| 7 | Path-specific projects | Speeches | Varies by pathway; complete all required projects |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I do the same role twice to satisfy requirements at the same level?**
Yes — at L4, you can "repeat a role above" as your optional fourth requirement. At L5, "repeat above" is one of the options in the fourth requirement group.

**Q: Do Table Topics speakers need to be pre-planned in the agenda?**
Yes — to count toward your Pathways progress, a Table Topics Speaker slot must appear in the meeting agenda so it can be tracked by the VPE. Impromptu floor participation does not count.

**Q: What is a "Specialized Role"?**
A club-specific supporting role such as Presiding Officer, Educational Presenter coordinating an external session, or a role approved by the VPE. Check with your VPE before assuming a Specialized Role will count.

**Q: What is an "Introductory Mentor"?**
You guide a new member (L0 or L1) through a single meeting — introducing them to the flow, roles, and culture. It is lighter than full mentorship. This is **not a meeting-agenda slot**; the VPE arranges the pairing and records it against your L3 progress. Contact the VPE to be assigned.

**Q: What is a "Club Mentor"?**
An ongoing mentorship role where you are assigned as a full mentor to a new member through their early levels. The VPE makes the assignment. It is **not a meeting-agenda slot** and is tracked separately from your meeting participation. It counts as the L5 optional role requirement.

**Q: Who validates my Educational Presentations in Base Camp?**
Your club VPE. Submit evidence in Base Camp after the presentation, then notify the VPE so they can approve it.
